# Packaging spec

It should feel like picking up the keys at a dealership, not like opening merch.

## The unboxing, in order

1. **Mailer**: plain matte black corrugated box with a small gold `OWN A CAR` on the tape. Nothing on the outside says what's inside.
2. **Sleeve**: black uncoated stock, slid over the box, gold foil `OWN A CAR` and `KEY Nº 0001 / ∞`.
3. **Rigid two-piece box** (lid and base, ~120 × 80 × 35 mm): soft-touch black wrap, blind-debossed
   key outline on the lid. The lid should lower slowly because of the air pressure.
4. **Inside the lid**: printed in gold, *"You now own a car."*
5. **Insert**: black velvet or flocked foam, cut to the key.
6. **The key**: cast from the real key in zinc alloy, plated brushed brass or gunmetal,
   with the key number laser-engraved on the bow. Weight is what makes it feel premium, so aim for 60–90 g.
7. **Certificate of ownership** (under the insert): 350 gsm cotton card, owner's name, key number, date,
   signature and a gold foil seal. It's printed from the order CSV.
8. **Card with a QR code** to the owners' Discord.

## Brand system (it matches the website)

- Colors: black `#0a0a0a`, bone `#f4efe6`, gold foil (≈ `#c9a24a`)
- Type: Fraunces Black for the wordmark, JetBrains Mono for numbers
- No manufacturer logos, wordmarks, shields or bull silhouettes anywhere

## Vendors to quote (get samples from at least two)

- Rigid boxes: Arka, Packlane, PakFactory, or custom rigid-box makers on Alibaba (cheapest; order samples first)
- Keys: custom die-cast / challenge-coin and keychain manufacturers. Send them 3D scans or photos of the real key.
- Certificates: a local print shop or Moo (foil)

Order a **sample of everything** before the presale opens, film the unboxing, and use that film as the launch video.
