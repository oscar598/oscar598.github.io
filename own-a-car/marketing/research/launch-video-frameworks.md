# OWN A CAR · Launch video frameworks

Research compiled 2026-09-29 for the ~Nov 9, 2026 launch on X, with TikTok and Reels as secondary platforms. The scripts at the end use only footage that already exists plus black Michroma title cards.

**How sure each claim is.** **[V]** means a published source reports it; the URL is in Sources. **[U]** means it isn't verified: it's from memory, one secondary source, or an estimate. Several primary pages (x.com, business.x.com, techcrunch, fastcompany, wikipedia, medium) were blocked by the research proxy. Numbers attributed to them come from search-engine extracts of those pages and are marked **[V*]**. Re-check any [V*] or [U] number before quoting it publicly.

---

## Summary

1. **Make the launch video 20–25 s and a loop-teaser 10–12 s.** On X, most reposted clips run 15–30 s. X's own ad guidance says under 15 s gets the highest completion rate. [V*] The open-sourced 2026 ranker reportedly gives watch-time credit only above a minimum video duration, but the exact value isn't published. [U] So don't make everything 6 s.
2. **Design for sound off.** 70–85% of X video impressions reportedly come from muted autoplay. [V*, third-party estimate] Twitter's own data said captioned videos get 28% more view time. [V*] Every beat needs to work as text on screen, and sound is a bonus layer.
3. **Put the hook in frame 1.** A view counts at 2 s with 50% of the player on screen (MRC standard). [V] Nothing in the 0–2 s window should be black, a logo or a slow fade. The spotlight reveal can't open the video; the key macro can.
4. **Lead with vertical, and keep a 16:9 master for X desktop and embeds.** X has had a full-screen vertical video tab since Jan 2025 and expanded it in Feb 2026. X says 100M users a day engage with vertical video there. [V*] Cut 9:16 first. The 16:9 version is a reframe, not the other way round.
5. **Upload natively and put the link in the first reply.** Musk said in Nov 2024 that link posts are deprioritized to stop "lazy linking". Third-party data shows link posts from regular accounts collapsing in reach. [V*] X was testing a softer link experience in 2025–26. [V*] Put the video in the post and the URL in a reply or pinned reply.
6. **Hold the deadpan, as MSCHF and Liquid Death do.** A 2026 audience reads it as deliberate. State facts, add no adjectives, and let the absurd mechanic do the work: "unlimited keys to one car", "the key doesn't start the car". The winning launches of the last three years did the same: one strange mechanic, stated plainly (Cluely, Friend, Duolingo, CAH).
7. **Avoid the counterexamples.** Long, low-energy founder videos flopped: Humane's 10-min video (2023) and the 9:21 io film (2025). Destruction imagery can backfire without a clear reading, as Apple's "Crush" did in 2024. The shatter shot must read as *being divided among people*, never as *destroying a car*.
8. **Label AI footage.** Jacquemus proved an 8-second CGI clip can go massively viral (2023), but the "is it real?" debate became the story. Our car is an AI render of a car not yet bought. Label it on screen ("CONCEPT RENDER"), in line with our own playbook rule. That turns a liability into part of the art: the only thing we don't have yet is the car.

## 2. Patterns (sourced and dated)

### 2.1 How X counts and ranks video
- **A view is 2 s at 50% of the player in view** (MRC standard), or a click to expand or unmute. Autoplay alone can trigger it. [V] *(superx.so / postplanify, 2026)* So the 0–2 s frames decide whether you get a view at all.
- **Watch time matters more under the 2026 Grok-based ranker.** xAI open-sourced the For You algorithm in **Jan 2026** (github.com/xai-org/x-algorithm) and promised updates every 4 weeks. [V*] Several teardowns say video watch time and dwell are weighted more heavily than before. A "video quality view" signal reportedly fires only above `MIN_VIDEO_DURATION_MS`. **The constant isn't published**, and one teardown uses ~60 s as a conservative guess. [U] Practical read: a 20–25 s hero video with high completion is the safe middle; don't chase 60 s.
- **The strongest positive predicted actions** are follow-from-feed, author-engaged replies and reposts. The strongest negatives are "Not interested", block, mute and report. [V*] *(2026 teardowns)* So reply to early comments in the first hour, and don't bait people into hide-worthy outrage.
- **Links:** Musk confirmed in Nov 2024 that posts with links in the main text are deprioritized ("lazy linking") and suggested putting links in replies. [V*] *(Mediagazer/Mediaite, 2024-11-25)* A third-party study reports link posts from non-Premium accounts near 0% engagement since Mar 2025. [V*] X head of product later tested a new iOS link experience. [V*] *(Tomorrow's Publisher, 2025)* **Rule:** native video in the post, URL in the first reply, pin the thread.

### 2.2 Length
- X ads guidance: **under 15 s for completion**, and put the key visual (logo, product, slogan) by the **3-second mark**. [V*] *(business.x.com creative best practices, retrieved via search 2026-09)*
- Organic benchmark: **15–30 s is the reposting sweet spot**. Under 15 s gets the most complete views but the least total watch time. 30–90 s suits demos and explainers. [V*] *(buzzvoice / tryclipup guides, 2026)*
- The 2024–26 hero-film norm is ≤60 s (Cybercab 55 s, Apple "Crush" 60 s). Long-form founder films (Humane 10 min, io 9:21) underperform as *videos* even when the news is big.
- Free X accounts upload up to 2:20; Premium goes much longer. [V] *(2026 spec guides)*

### 2.3 Sound off, captions, sound design
- **70–85% of X video impressions are muted autoplay** (2026 third-party estimate). [V*] Twitter's own study: videos that work without sound get **+28% view time** and **1.8× ROI**. [V*] *(Twitter business blog, via yansmedia 2025)*
- **Burn in text.** Don't rely on X's auto-captions; they miss brand names. [V*]
- X's **vertical video tab and full-screen player play with sound on by default** in the ad context. [V*] So the sound bed matters there: design it for people who unmute or land in the video tab.
- Sound design that survives both: **foley over music**. Metal-on-metal key clicks, a spotlight "thunk", a brass cascade, a glass-granular shatter, silence before the end card. Licensed music is a risk on X and a rights problem for cross-posting. TikTok and Reels versions can swap in a trending sound in-app.

### 2.4 First frame and thumbnail
- The first frame *is* the thumbnail in the feed. Rules: **no black frame, no logo sting, no fade-in.** Open on the strongest object (the brass key) with the first line of text already on screen.
- Show movement in the first second. [V*] *(X creative guidance)*
- MrBeast guide (leaked Sep 2024): the opening must immediately prove what the thumbnail promised. [V] *(Tubefilter, 2024-09-17)*
- TikTok: 63% of the highest-CTR videos hook within 3 s (TikTok for Business, via secondary). [V*] Holding 70–85% of viewers at 3 s correlates with ~2.2× total views (2025 industry data, single source). [U]

### 2.5 Pacing (cuts per second)
- TV commercials: 2–5 s shots. Scenes held **≥1.5 s are better recognized**, so faster than that hurts memory. [V] *(Camera Shot Length in TV Commercials, research summary)*
- Top YouTube Shorts in 2024: an **average clip of ~2.5 s** correlated with 35% higher completion than 4 s+ clips. [V*] *(OpusClip analysis, single source)*
- **For us:** a 1.5–3 s shot rhythm, with **one long held beat (~3–4 s)** on the spotlight reveal and one on the shatter. That's roughly 0.4 cuts per second, which fits deadpan. A cut every 0.5 s would break the luxury tone.

### 2.6 On-screen text conventions
- One line per card, **≤7 words**, sentence-case facts in ALL CAPS Michroma, full stops. Hold each line **≥1.5 s** (readable at ~3–4 words per second).
- Keep text in the centre safe area. For 9:16, keep text out of the **top ~12% and bottom ~20%**: X, TikTok and Reels UI (caption, buttons) overlays those zones. [U, standard practice]
- Label provenance: **CONCEPT RENDER** on the AI car and **SCREEN RECORDING · OWNACAR** on the site physics. Figure's "1.0x speed, no cuts" label is the precedent for pre-empting "fake" replies.
- Banned words on screen and in captions (from our PLAYBOOK): investment, return, profit, stake, shares, appreciate, worth more later, win, lucky, raffle, giveaway.

### 2.7 Loopability
- Twitter historically auto-looped videos **under 6.5 s** (2017). [V] On mobile since 2024, reaching the end can auto-advance to another video instead of looping. [V*, user report] So loops can't be *relied on* on X. They still help on TikTok and Reels, where replays add watch time.
- Build loops with a **matched first and last frame** (key macro → key macro) and no end-card hold. Put the URL in the post text, not the last frame.

### 2.8 Vertical vs horizontal on X (2025–2026)
- **Jan 2025:** X launched a dedicated vertical video tab (US first), then rolled it out globally (India, Australia, Europe). [V]
- **Feb 18, 2026:** a new update pushed vertical video further with an immersive full-screen player. [V*] *(TechCrunch 2026-02-18, via search)*
- X: **100M users a day** engage with vertical video; vertical ads add **~10% incremental reach**. [V*] An older Twitter stat: vertical gets **2.5× engagement** over horizontal. [V*, dated]
- **Recommendation:** post **9:16 on X from phones-first accounts**. Keep a **16:9** version for replies, press, embeds and YouTube. Where the UI crop matters, a **4:5** feed cut is a compromise. [U]

### 2.9 Staging
- Nothing's staged reveals (teaser → BTS → launch) and Duolingo's two-week arc both stayed in the news cycle for weeks. [V]
- Kickstarter: projects with a video fund at **~50–54%** versus **~30–39%** without. [V*] The all-or-nothing format rewards video.

## 3. The framework: "State. Show. Divide. Name. Count."

Five beats for every OWN A CAR video, at any length:

| Beat | Job | Footage | Rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1 · STATE** (0–2 s) | Say the absurd fact while the key is on screen | Brass key macro | Text on frame 1. No black, no logo |
| **2 · SHOW** | Prove the object | Spotlight reveal of the Gallardo (label: CONCEPT RENDER) | The one long, held, luxurious shot |
| **3 · DIVIDE** | Show the mechanic: unlimited keys, one car | Key rain, then the shatter slider 1 → 100,000 | Always pair destruction with a count ("OWNERS: 100,000") |
| **4 · NAME** | Make it personal | Name wrap and loupe; certificate; unboxing | Show *one* name large. The loupe is the "that could be me" beat |
| **5 · COUNT** | Close with stakes and the rule | Black card: 6,000 OR REFUND · date · OWN A CAR | No CTA verbs like "Buy now". State the rule and the date |

**Style constants:** black and brass, Michroma for all titles, captions in the same face at smaller size, all caps, full stops. No voiceover in A and B. Foley only. No smiles, no exclamation marks, no emojis in the video.

**Posting mechanics (X):**
1. Native upload of the 9:16 at 1080×1920, H.264/AAC, 30 fps. Upload the 16:9 1920×1080 as the reply or the press version.
2. Post text without a link. The link goes in the first reply from @ownacar, and Oscar quote-posts from his account.
3. For the first 60 minutes, reply to every substantive question with a fact or card. Author replies are among the highest-weighted signals.
4. Stagger: B-variants on the days before launch, A on launch day, C after the first milestone (or on day 2 to answer "why?").

## 4. Scripts

Footage codes:
- **KEY**: brass key macro, 5 s
- **CAR**: AI spotlight reveal of the white Gallardo, 8 s. Always super "CONCEPT RENDER".
- **BOX**: unboxing, 8 s
- **RAIN**: website screen recording, keys raining and piling up
- **SHATTER**: website screen recording, car shattering into one shard per owner, slider 1 → 100,000
- **WRAP**: website screen recording, car wrapped in owners' names, with a loupe
- **S-BOX**, **S-CERT**, **S-CROWD**: stills of the box, the certificate and the crowd holding keys (use slow 3–5% push-ins)
- **CARD**: black title card, Michroma, brass text (#B08D57-ish) or white

### Script C: Founder video, "Why I'm doing this" (40 s)

**Talking-head plan:**
- **Set:** Oscar in front of seamless black, with a single hard key light at 45° and slight brass-coloured rim or background glow. Locked-off camera at eye level; phone on a tripod is fine. Shoot **native 9:16 at 4K**, which also yields a 16:9 crop from the same take if framed with headroom.
- **Wardrobe:** black. Prop: one brass key, in hand or on a finger, visible in every take.
- **Delivery:** deadpan, no smiling, slightly slower than normal. Look into the lens. One sentence per take; jump-cut between sentences and never cover a jump cut with a zoom punch-in more than once.
- **Audio:** lav or phone close-mic, 30 s of room tone recorded. No music under the voice. Foley ducks under dialogue.
- **Captions:** burned in, sentence at a time, in the centre-lower third, Michroma at a smaller size (or a neutral grotesk for legibility), all caps, max two lines.
- **Shoot three alternative hooks** (listed below) and A/B them as separate posts on different days.
- **B-roll** cutaways cover roughly 40% of runtime, so the viewer is never on a static face for more than ~6 s.

| Time | Shot / footage | Dialogue (Oscar, to camera) = burned-in caption | Sound |
