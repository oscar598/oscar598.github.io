# OWN A CAR · fact sheet

| | |
|---|---|
| **What** | An art project: unlimited $50 keys to one Lamborghini. Every key holder is an owner |
| **Artist** | Oscar Lu |
| **Price** | $50 per key (US shipping included; +$15 international). Up to 10 per order |
| **Keys available** | Unlimited |
| **Goal** | 6,000 keys by Dec 9, 2026, 11:59 pm ET, all or nothing, with automatic refunds if missed |
| **The car** | Used Lamborghini Gallardo (2006–08). Stretch goals: Huracán at 14,000 keys, Aventador at 24,000 |
| **What owners get** | Numbered solid-brass key cast from the real key, rigid gift box, signed certificate, their name on the car's wrap, a private key-tracking page, the owners' Discord |
| **What owners don't get** | Legal title, profit, resale proceeds, the right to drive. It's art, not an investment |
| **The name wrap** | The car is wrapped in every owner's name. At 6,000 owners each name is ~18 mm tall |
| **Your share** | 1,430 kg ÷ owners. At 6,000: 238 g (a phone). At 100,000: 14 g (a lug nut) |
| **Where the $50 goes** | $20.45 car · $13.00 key/box/certificate · $8.80 tax reserve · $6.00 shipping · $1.75 card fees |
| **Ships** | March–April 2027 |
| **After** | The car is never sold for profit. Owners vote on where it's shown. If it ever leaves the project, it's donated |
