## Artist sells unlimited $50 keys to one Lamborghini: "Everyone who buys one owns it"

*OWN A CAR asks what ownership means when there's no limit on owners. If 6,000 keys sell by Dec 9, the car is bought and wrapped in every owner's name. If not, everyone is refunded.*

**PRINCETON, N.J., Nov 10, 2026.** Artist and designer Oscar Lu today opened the presale for *OWN A CAR*, an art project
that sells an unlimited number of $50 keys to a single Lamborghini. Every buyer receives a numbered, solid-brass key cast
from the car's real key, a signed certificate of ownership, and their name on the car itself: when the presale closes,
the Lamborghini will be wrapped bumper to bumper in the names of every owner.

The catch is the math. With 100 owners, each owns 14 kilograms of car. With 6,000, each owns 238 grams, roughly a phone.
At 100,000 owners, a key is worth about a lug nut of Lamborghini. The site's live calculator shows each visitor
exactly how much car their $50 buys at the current count, and how tall their name will be on the wrap.

"Ownership usually means one name on a title and one set of keys," said Lu. "This asks what happens when the keys are
unlimited. Every new owner makes the car more owned and every owner less of an owner. At some number, a Lamborghini
belongs to everybody, and to nobody."

The presale is all or nothing. If 6,000 keys aren't sold by 11:59 pm ET on Dec 9, 2026, every order is refunded
automatically. The project publishes its books: of each $50, $20.45 goes to the car, $13 to the key, box and certificate,
$8.80 to taxes, $6 to shipping and $1.75 to card fees. Stretch goals upgrade the car to a Huracán at 14,000 keys and an
Aventador at 24,000.

Keys carry no financial interest. There are no returns, no resale payouts and no right to drive the car, which will
never be sold for profit. Owners join a Discord where they vote on where the car is shown and on owners' days.
