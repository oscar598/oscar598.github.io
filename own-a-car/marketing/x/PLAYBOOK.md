# X playbook

**The one job:** get the launch thread quoted, argued about and screenshotted, and turn that attention into
keys before the presale closes. Every post here is designed to be a screenshot, a debate or a milestone.

Posts ready to copy: `posts.md`. Images: `cards/*.png` (rendered) and `../../assets/*` (Runway).
Tracking sheet: `tracker.csv`.

---

## 1. Why this can spread

| **Polls** | `posts.md` § Polls | Cheap engagement that feeds the algorithm and the argument |

## 5. Daily presale routine (≈45 minutes)

1. **Morning:** check the site counter (the registry syncs every 15 min). Screenshot anything funny from the owners' wall.
2. **Post the plan for the day** from `posts.md` (one counter, one fact/joke, one owner repost, one reply session).
3. **20 minutes of replies:** find the day's big posts about Lamborghinis, supercars, "being rich", art-world stunts and
   money memes. Reply with something that adds a joke plus the calculator screenshot. **Never** "check out my project", never
   the same reply twice, max ~10 replies/day from @ownacar. Oscar can be more personal.
4. **Repost owners** posting their certificate or name (search `#OwnACar` and `ownacar`).
5. **8:00 pm ET:** the daily count post (same time every day turns it into a show).
6. Log numbers in `tracker.csv`.

## 7. Seeding: who to send it to (DM templates)

Categories, not names. Pick 10–20 from each that you'd genuinely be happy to be quote-posted by:

1. **Car X:** supercar spotters, car-meme accounts, car YouTubers who post on X.
2. **Art and internet-culture X:** people who cover stunts, net art, "this is art?" discourse.
3. **Money and meme X:** accounts that do "the math" jokes and absurd-finance takes. Careful: ask them to frame it as art, not a deal.
4. **Your own circle:** Princeton, your design community (2,000 members), anyone who's known you since the eBay days.
5. **Press** (see `../press/`), after the first 24 h have numbers to report.

**DM template (friends with audiences):**
> I'm launching an art project today: unlimited $50 keys to one Lamborghini, and everyone who buys one owns it. All or nothing.
> The launch thread's here: [link]. A quote-post with your honest take (even "this is insane") would mean a lot.

**DM template (people you don't know):**
> Made something I think your timeline would argue about: I'm selling unlimited keys to one Lamborghini. $50 each, everyone's an
> owner, refund if we miss the goal. [link to thread]. No ask beyond: if it's your kind of thing, quote it.

Never offer money or free keys for posts without an FTC `#ad` disclosure. If you gift a key to a creator, they must disclose it.

## 7b. From the research (marketing/research/)

- **Pre-sell the first 100–300 keys privately** (friends, classmates, early DMs) so they land at launch minute and the
  first public count isn't "3". Momentum drives momentum (ConstitutionDAO, Exploding Kittens).
- **Root post = native video, no link.** The link goes in the first self-reply. Author replies in the first two hours are the
  strongest known ranking signal. Staff them.
- **Space @ownacar posts 2–4 hours apart.** Use self-replies for rapid updates (author-diversity decay).
- **Countdown posts carry the most weight.** Backing accelerates near a goal. They're in the queue as C3–C0.
- **Never ask for engagement** ("reply for a follow", "RT to…"). X removes accounts for it in 2026.
- **Name the MSCHF lineage yourself** (see `OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`).

## 8. Paid (optional)

- **$300–$500 promoted boost** on the launch thread on day 1, targeting followers of car and art accounts. Only boost a post that's
  already doing well organically.
- No follower buying, no engagement pods, no bot replies: X suppresses it, and it poisons the "is this real" argument.

## 9. Rules that protect the project

- Never: *investment, return, profit, stake, shares, appreciate, worth more later, win, lucky, raffle, giveaway.*
- Label AI images as **concept renders** until the real key and box exist.
- No Lamborghini logo, crest or wordmark in any image. The name in text is fine.
- Never fake a number. The counter is the credibility.
- Giveaways of keys are a legal minefield (sweepstakes law). Don't run any without the lawyer.

## 10. Measuring

Log in `tracker.csv` daily: followers (both accounts), impressions, profile visits, link clicks (X analytics), site visits
(if analytics are added), keys sold (site counter). Two numbers matter: **link clicks → keys** (site conversion; if it's below 2%,
fix the page) and **keys/day** (if it's flat 3 days in a row, change the content, not the volume).
