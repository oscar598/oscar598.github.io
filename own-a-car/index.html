      Straight answers

      Do I actually own the car?

      You own a numbered key, your certificate, your name on the car and your place in the piece: ownership as an artwork. The legal title stays with the project, because a DMV title can't hold thousands of names and because a legal share would make this an investment, which it isn't.

      Is this an investment?

      No. There is no return, no dividend, no resale payout and no promise that a key will be worth anything later. Buy one because you want to be in it.

      Is it a raffle? Does one key start the car?

      No. Every buyer gets exactly the same thing. Nothing is random. The keys are sculptures and don't start anything.

      What if the goal isn't reached?

      Every order, including shipping and tax, is refunded in full to the card you paid with, within 10 days of the deadline. You don't need to do anything.

      Can I drive it?

      No. Owners' days, where owners can see the car and sit in it, are planned once it's bought. Owners vote in Discord on where they happen.

      What happens to the car after?

      It's never sold for profit. It's kept, shown and toured as the artwork. If it ever has to leave the project, it will be donated, and owners will be told first.

      When does my key arrive?

      After the presale closes and the car is bought.

      Can I buy more than one?

      Up to 10 per order. Each is its own numbered key.

      Is this affiliated with Lamborghini?

      No. The car is the subject of the artwork. The project isn't affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Automobili Lamborghini.
