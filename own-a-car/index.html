Straight answers

Do I legally own part of the car?

No. You're buying an artwork: a numbered key and your place in the piece. The car's title stays with the project. Keys carry no share of the car, no resale proceeds and no right to drive it.

Is this an investment?

No. There's no return, no dividend and no promise that the key will be worth anything later. Buy it because you want the key.

Does the key start the car?

No. It's cast from the real key but it's a sculpture, not a working key.

What if the goal isn't reached?

Every buyer is refunded in full to their original payment method within 10 days of the deadline.

When does it ship?

After the presale closes and the car is bought.
