Only your key number(s), your purchase date and the wall name you chose, if any. Nothing else about you is ever published. Your private key page is found by a code only your checkout link carries.

What we use it for

Who we share it with

Stripe (payments and tax), our email provider, our shipping label provider and carrier, and a fulfilment partner if we use one, each only for the job above. We never sell or rent your data.

How long we keep it

Order records for as long as tax law requires (generally 7 years). Everything else is deleted 12 months after your Key is delivered or refunded. Email us to ask for a copy, a correction or deletion of your data.
